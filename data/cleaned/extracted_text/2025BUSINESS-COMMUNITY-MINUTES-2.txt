iii. Challenges faced during the FY 2024  
▪ Delayed disbursement of the CDF consequently resulting in delay in the 
implementation of the projects 
▪  Delayed disbursement of in Road Grant  resulting in delayed 
implementation. 
Page | 5  
 
▪ Delayed disbursement of  the devolution support grant consequently 
resulting in delay in implementation. 
▪ The ministry guided that we budget for 10 million for the component of 
grant in lieu of rates, but at a later stage, it was guided that government 
could not fund us that amount therefore affecting the operations of the 
council. Leading to adjustment of the budget  downwards by the 
mentioned amount. This meant that all projects that were budgeted in 
the amount were to be suspended 
Plenary 
Stakeholders wanted to know why the Local Authority delayed in finishing the implementation 
of the CDF 2023 projects and why the desks why not procured. 
In response, the Director of Finance indicated that the initiation of the procurem ent process 
delayed a bit in starting. Hence, contractors possessed the sites late. However, works were 
progressing well. Regarding the procurement of desks, the Director informed the stakeholders 
that stated that the procurement of desks was phased, hence, the Local Authority could not buy 
all the needed desks at the same time. 
Further, the stakeholders expressed concern regarding the limited participation of local 
contractors in the implementation of CDF projects.  
In response the Director of Finance indicated that it was noted that there was low awareness 
amongst the local contractors on the use of the e -GP. Hence, sensitization meetings had been 
lined up to bridge this gap.

Proposed projects for 2025 
The Socio-economic Planner informed the members present that a number of applications were 
received from the community. However, resources were limited hence projects to be 
implemented in the FY 2025 would include:  
1. Procurement of Earth moving equipment 
2. Rehabilitation of Fungulwe feeder road 
3. Construction of Classroom Blocks 
4. Procurement of desks 
5. Construction of Milulu Health Post 
6. Drilling of commercial borehole Mukutuma Rural Health Centre 
7. Procurement of medical equipment  
 
Plenary 
The members indicated that the consideration to construct classroom blocks, health post and 
medical equipment was a welcome move. However, the Local Authority was urged to consider 
buying a borehole drilling rig because the water shortage was the greatest challenge the District 
was facing.  
In response, the Socio-economic planner indicated that the observation by the members was 
correct. However, resources were limited to execute all the projects at the same time. Hence, 
the proposal would be considered before 2026. 
Further, the participants urged the Local Authority to also consider the construction of crossing 
points in Sonkontwe ward.
