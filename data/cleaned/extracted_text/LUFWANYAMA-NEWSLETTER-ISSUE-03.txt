L
ufwanyama Town Council in col -
laboration with the District Com -
missioner’s office has launched 
the Cash for Work (CFW) programme 
a Government initiative in response to 
the drought experienced in 2023/2024 
farming season.
Speaking during the launch of the 
(CFW), Lufwanyama member of Parlia -
ment Kenny Siachisumo, has called on 
WDCs and Councillors to work togeth -
er during this initiative as the guidelines 
are clear of what the role of the WDCs 
are, and that of the Councillors respec -
tively.
Mr Siachisomu added that the Cash for 
Work programme is yet another initi -
ative by the government to help those 
affected by the drought in all affected 
constituencies .
And Guest of honor District Commis -
sioner Justin Mwalikwa, stated that the 
Cash for Work (CFW) Programme aims 
to provide temporal relief to drought-af-
fected communities and urban areas im-
pacted by food price increases and job 
losses. This will be done by offering cash 
in exchange for labor, the programme 
empowers individuals to improve imme-
diate access to food, thereby alleviating 
vulnerable communities and supporting 
their well-being during this challenging 
time by Stimulating economic activity at 
community level.
Mr. Mwalikwa added that the pro -
gramme demonstrates a proactive ap -
proach in addressing the consequences 
of drought and economic hardship, by 
supporting individuals and communities 
in need, the Government is helping to 
mitigate the effects of these challenges 
and promote self reliance.
The Cash for Work (CFW) programme 
is specifically designed to reach vulner-
able households in areas not already 
covered by existing social cash transfer 
programs (SCT and ECT).

INDEPENDENCE TOURNAMENT
CONSTITUENCY DEVEOPMENT FUND
EMPOWERING PEOPLE     TRANSFORMING COMMUNITIES
C D FCOMMUNITY LOCAL NEWS
L
ufwanyama Town Council held it's 
first ever independence tourna -
ment play offs for all the 20 wards 
in the district, this was in line with the 
National Sports Policy.
The purpose of the National Sports 
Policy was to provide a practical frame-
work for sports development in Zambia. 
It was a plan and statement of intent that 
offers guidance and advice on how to 
accomplish the development objectives, 
goals, and priorities in order to raise the 
profile of sports in all parts of the coun-
try.
The policy was re-launched on the 
13th September 2024 and aimed to 
create sustainable, inclusive and robust 
sporting environment that maximizes 
the potential of every citizen to achieve 
the 2030 sustainable development goals, 
which recognizes the role of sport in 
achieving the Sustainable Development 
Goals (SDGs).
As a result, local authorities will now 
be responsible for establishing mul -
ti-purpose sports facilities, identifying 
and nurturing talent, registering sports 
associations, enforcing regulations, and 
implementing the National Sports Policy 
at district level.
With this said, Lufwanyama is hold -
ing the District tournament which was 
kicked off early this month with the Si -
mi-finals held today 19th October 2024 
and finals being held tomorrow 20th 
October 2024 respectively.
The tournament has seen Katembula 
ward and Chibanga ward qualify to the 
finals to be played tomorrow while Kaf-
ubu ward and Bulaya ward will square it 
out for the third and fourth positions on 
the same day.
The final games will be officiated by 
Lufwanyama member of Parliament Ken-
ny Siachisomu at Shimukunami Com -
bined School ground.
