L
UFWANYAMA District, is ap-
proximately 130 Km’s from 
Ndola, the Provincial Capital 
and 455 Km’s from Lusaka, the Cap-
ital of Zambia. Lufwanyama is bor -
dered by Kalulushi, Kitwe, Masaiti, 
Mpongwe, Luanshya, Mushindamo, 
Chingola Districts.
Lufwanyama was part of Ndola Rural 
District, until its establishment by an Or-
der of the President in 1997. 
This was necessitated by the vastness 
of Ndola rural and the need to bring  
service delivery closer to the local 
communities. Ndola Rural District was 
then split into Lufwanyama, Masaiti and 
Mpongwe Districts.
The District consists of one constituen-
cy and 20 wards. 
The major tribes in Lufwanyama are 
Lamba and Kaonde, with Lamba being the 
predominant language of communication.  
Traditional communities are organ -
ized on the basis of villages often led 
by village headmen/women. Traditional  
authority is vested in Chiefdoms. 
There are Six chiefs namely, Chieftainess 
Shimukunani, Chief Nkana, Chief Muku -
tuma, Chief Shibuchunga, Chief Fungulwe 
and Chief Lumpuma. The District has a 
population of 114,768 with an estimated 
annual growth rate of 2.2 per cent.

the challenges faced.
Mr Kapata visited among others; Chine-

the community and the Immediate ben -

move by the Government through CDF

with the mobility of patients from the 
health post as it lacks an ambulance.

munication towers has made it challeng-
ing for them to communicate during 
referrals from the health post to the 
District hospital.
Speaking at Chinemu Health Post, 
Deputy Council Chairperson and Ward 
Councilor Gilbert Mukobola is happy 
that his ward benefited from CDF , he

ward of improving the health post by in-
cluding all the necessary facilities in the 
next CDF budget allocation.
And Lufwanyama Boarding Headteach-
er School Mrs. Melody Chinoya, appre -
ciated the move by the Government for 
introducing free education, which the 
school currently has 103 pupils under 
CDF school boarding bursary under 
Lufwanyama T own Council and 124 from 
around the country. Mrs. Chinoya adds 
that, the school also benefited 230 desks 
under CDF which have greatly helped 
the school.
And speaking during the visit at Milulu 
heath post, Katembula Ward Councilor 
Edward Kapandula thanked the Govern-
ment for increasing CDF which has seen 
the implementation of more projects in 
the District, which he adds is a plus for 
the people of his Ward.
Meanwhile Lufwanyama T own Coun -
cil Secretary Lynda Mapara assured the 
people of the district that the council 
is on top of things where CDF is con -
cerned, for the proper utilization of the 
funds.

COUNCIL CONTINUES WITH 
THE DELIVERY OF DESKS
E
ducation being the best equal-
ise for both the poor and rich 
is one thing that can and will 
best improve the face of the coun -
try province, streaming down to 
the district.
T o provide quality education Lufanyama 
district needs to provide the best edu -
cation facilities which should start from 
the provision of quality learning environ-
ment. with this said, the government has 
directed all councils in the country to 
ensure no child seats on the floor in all 
schools in the country.
This has put all councils in charge of 
procuring desks to cushion the shortage 
in school by the end of 2024 which is a 
presidential directive.
It is for this that Lufwanyama T own 
Council has continued with the desk 
distribution in the district to ensure no 
child seats on the floor by December 
2024.
Currently, the district has a shortage of 
2,221 from the initial deficit of 13,681 in 
2023, which has seen the local authori -
ty procure 11,460 desks to cushion the 
deficit.
In addition, 462 desks were rehabili -
tated through the ministry of education 
initiative spearheaded by the Headquar-
ters.
The impact of desks has been evident 
in the  schools in the district as the local 
authority closes in on the shortage of 
desks in the district.
For newly built classroom blocks under 
the constituency development fun CDF , 
the Council ensures that desks are deliv-
ered to the schools as part of the build-
ing project of the schools in the district.
Lufwanyama T own Council will ensure 
the 2221 is covered by the end of the 
year 2024 as of the presidential directive.
