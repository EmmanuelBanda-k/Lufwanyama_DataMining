LUFYWANYAMA TOWN COUNCIL LEADERSHIP 
As your Area Member of Parliament for Lufwanyama I am de-
lighted to inform the great people of Lufwanyama of the com-
mitment I have for the development of our district. 
 
With the continued increase of CDF and the commitment of my 
office together with the council, we will endeavour to foster 
development in the district and ensure we develop the city, as 
we are in a hurry to foster development and see to it that the 
constituency development fund benefits all the people of                   
Lufwanyama. 
 
In line with President Hakainde Hichilema and the vision of the 
United Party for National Development (UPND) which is to 
bring development down to the people, by making you the peo-
ple of the district choose what you want from the money alloca-
tion of CDF, I see to it that that goal is achieved.  
 
I wish to register to you that the goal for decentralization is        
being achieved as seen from what has been achieved in the dis-
trict from the time in office. 
 
And also wish to state that I will continue to ensure the District 
and the people of Lufwanyama come first. 
. 
1 
 
As we continue to upgrade our District with hard work 
and dedication, as your Council Chairperson for the great 
city of Lufwanyama. It is with great honour that together 
with our cooperate partners we continue to put the inter-
ests of the district first. 
 
Being a District that is rich with fertile land for farming 
and mineral wealth, which puts the district on the map for 
much more development than it has, it is with great               
expectations that I want the district to live up to the rich-
es it has through infrastructure and social                       
development. 
 
We believe as the Council that Lufwanyama can be 
greater than it is, and must achieve the standard worth of 
a developed district as it has all it needs to be a great              
District. 
 
I therefore call on every well meaning citizen of Lufwan-
yama to work for the development of the District, for our 
future generation as we should not remain rich only on 
paper, but let the rich land of Lufwanyama show from 
our hard work. 
Hon. Kenny Siachisumo,  
Lufwanyama Member of Parliament 
H.W Annie Kapandula– Kalamatila 
Lufwanyama Council Chairperson

WELCOME TO LUFWANYAMA TOWN COUNCIL 
 
Lufwanyama Town Council  is one council which has strived to ensure the 
district attains the much needed development for the people of the district. 
The council, through the constituency development fund CDF has ensured 
that people from far and near within the district benefit from the k25.7 mil-
lion kwacha. 
 
With the increased funds of CDF, it has been the coal responsibility of the 
council to see to it that funds from the council are properly utilized and                   
ensure the prime purpose of the money is the end goal. 
 
The District is  one that has seen much development through the hard work 
of the team at the council since the district was declared a District in 1997 as 
compared to other districts. 
 
The council is working to  ensure the development of the district, in line with 
the mandate of government through decentralisation and devolution of              
government.

istering a Club/Cooperative: 
   
Step No. 1 
 Obtain an application form 
 Complete the application form 
 Attach your club/Cooperative constitution 
 Attach minutes of your meeting 
 Attach certified copies of all members’ NRCs 
Note: Form can be collected from the    Department 
of Planning 
 
Step No.  
 Pay the K200 registration fee 
 Pay through Cashier’s Office or the bank (Ask for 
bank details) 
 Attach your receipt to the application form as you 
submit 
 
Step No. 3 
 The Community Development Officer will scrutinise 
all submitted documents to make sure that everything 
is according to standard requirements. 
 A certificate will be issued if everything is correct

YOUTHS AT KATEMBULA SKILLS CENTER UNDER CDF SKILLS TRAINING PROGRAM            
UNDER THE CDF SKILLS DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM 
Lufwanyama Town Council paid for 137 students under 2022 
CDF,  this number has since increased with the increase in the 
CDF allocation introduced by government. Which has seen 
tremendous response from the community with the number of 
people applying under various categories of CDF. 
 
Lufwanyama Town Council Remains ready and able to ensure the 
money reaches the intended beneficiaries . 
 
 
The Council  continues to work with the community and ensure the growth and development of the 
people is attained through community engagements in areas that are for the benefit of the community.  
 
Following a concern by the public over the growing number of children patronising drinking places in 
the district, the Council through the health department conducted a sensitization program in schools to 
teach children about the dangers of alcohol abuse, in partnership with the Police .

Lufwanyama Town Council Health Department conducting a joint sensitization with Police at Katembula market  
With the growing number of under age drinking in the district which has  been coupled with the lack of            rec-
reation facilities in the district, bar owners in the district remain the key in ensuring they health both                
themselves and the police to avoid finding themselves on the wrong side of the law. 
 
Both the council through the health department and police, continue to outline the risk of having under age chil-
dren in there premises for whatever reason, as it is against the liquor licensing Act. 
 
The Bar owners have been encouraged to act according to the laws established to avoid being closed down if 
found wanting. 
 
                                 OUR GOAL AS A COUNCIL 
As a Council, our vision is to ensure that equitable and sustainable development is delivered to all parts of the District, i n line 
with the Governments desire of not leaving anyone behind . Together with other cooperating companies, the district has to 
work hand in head to develop the district. 
 
Lufwanyama District being the home of the emerald mines in the country, it is important to see to it that much is 
done to attract local investment as the District still struggles with the challenge of accommodation for different de-
partments in the district the local authority inclusive. 
 
This challenge has forced many working in the District to live in the nearby district of Kalulushi, Kitwe and Lu-
anshya respectively.
