3.0   NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT PLAN FRAMEWORK
Lufwanyama Town Council operates within its mandate to deliver local public goods to the people in the 
District and to discharge all or any functions set out in Article 147 (2) (c) of the Constitution of Zambia 
and Local Government amendment Act No 2 of 2019. The Council aims to improve the quality of lives 
for the people of Lufwanyama through the provision of efficient, effective, high quality, equitable and 
sustainable public services in a transparent and accountable manner with full stakeholder participation.
Lufwanyama Town Council aims to contribute significantly in the development of the District through 
the provision of efficient, effective, sustainable, public services and environmentally sound public goods 
by fostering community participation in development and  enhancing trade and commerce in the district 
through robust infrastructure development. Further, The Local Authority intends to improve access to 
clean water through increasing the number of boreholes to significant levels so that the people of 
Lufwanyama  have access to Clean water, a well-organized sanitation which is in line with the Council 
Strategic Plan and the 8TH National Development Plan.

4.0   BUDGET SUMMARY
Lufwanyama Town Council will embark on pursuing the Strategic Development Areas (SDA) espounsed 
by the Eighth National Development Plan (8NDP) in fulfilling the mandate as set out in the 2024 Local 
Authority Budget Estimates standing at K83.5 million. This represents an increase of 32 percent from the 
2023 budget of K60.5 million. The increase in the budget is attributed to the Government increasing the 
Constituency Development Fund (CDF) from K28.3 million to K30.6 million. The Local Authority also 
received  K4.9 million from devolved ministries and K3.7 million from Road Transport and Safety Agency 
(RATSA). It is essential to note also that a grant worth K3.72 million ($200,000) from the Zambia 
Devolution Support Programme (ZDSP) for capital projects and personal emoluments has also 
contributed to the increase of the total budget. 
The 2024 budget amounts will go towards the fulfillment of its mandate through the implementation of 
thirteen programmes that are currently relevent for implementation Lufwanyama. Note that Transport, 
Pontoon and Logistics has not been bugdeted for implementation.

The Constituency Development Programme has been allocated a total of 30.6 million representing 38% 
of the total budget  which is spread among the following Sub-Programmes: Community Projects Sub-
Programme (K17.4 million); Youth/Women  Empowerment Sub-Programme (5.8million); Secondary 
School and Skills Development Bursaries (K5.8 million); and lastly, K1.5 million as Administrative 
Component. These are aimed at improving the social economic structure of the district and improve 
some of the basic public infrastructure.
	
Local Governance Programme has been allocated K4 million to be used under the following sub 
programmes: Legislative Function sub-programme (K2.9 million), and Citizen Engagement sub-
programme (K1.1 million). These programme/s will ensure council committees are in place and 
operational, and also ensure that WDC elections are held and operationalized.
The Integrated Development Plan (IDP) programme has been allocated a budget of K3.1 million, with 
K215, 161.00 specifically earmarked for environmental planning programs, including climate change 
mitigation. Spatial planning gets K1.5 million for land management activities and K1.3 million for social 
economic activities.
As part of this plan, the council intends to develop one Local Area Plan (LAP) to facilitate coordinated 
development efforts. In order to ensure inclusiveness in governance matters, the council is committed 
to active stakeholder engagement throughout the planning and implementation phases. Furthermore, 
the council has ambitious plans to conduct developmental engagement meetings with stakeholders in all 
20 wards of the district. These interactions aim to gather diverse perspectives and input, fostering a 
collaborative approach to sustainable and inclusive development within the community
Under the Public Health and Environmental Protection programme, the council has allocated funds 
totaling K1.9 million. Specifically,K52,341.00 will be channeled towards Cemetery and funeral services, 
K391,692  is earmarked for health inspection activities, and K374,188 is allocated towards pest control 
initiatives. Additionally, Solid Waste Management and other activities related to environmental 
protection such has Keep Zambia Clean, Green and Health Campaign, and the management of dump 
sites has been allocated a sum of K477, 343.00. Furthermore, within the same programme, the council 
has allocated K678, 908.00 for the water and sanitation services. This strategic allocation reflects the 
council's commitment to enhancing public health, environmental sustainability, and community well-
being. The distribution of funds across these various activities underscores the comprehensive approach 
taken by the council to address both health and environmental challenges within the community.
Housing and Community Amenities programme has the total allocation amounting to K11 million under 
the following sub-programmes; Public Housing sub-programme (K4.2 million), Roads and Drainages sub-
programme (K6.2 million) and Street Lighting sub-programme (K595, 000.00). The programmes are 
going to ensure the council delivers quality services in developing and maintaining a pristine 
environment, amongst others renovating of Council houses, Construction of fire offices and completing 
some rooms at the council lodge. Not forgetting construction of proper all weather gravel roads.
Recreation Culture and Religion programme will be allocated K917, 378.00 apportioned to the Sports 
Promotion sub-programme to help organize and coordinate sporting and culture activities in the district.

Community Projects
Lufwanyama Town Council has planned to take a huge step in overcoming the district's greatest 
challenge: Road Network. Most of the Community Project allocation has been set apart for the purchase 
of a Grader and a Lowbed Truck. The remaining amount has been planned to be spent on procurement 
of a Chief Retainer's Motorcycle, School Desks and an Ambulance according to the Presidential Priorities 
under 2024. 
Disaster Contingency
Community Projects consist of a special kind called Disaster Projects. This is an allocation of 5 percent of 
Community Projects and in 2024 amounts to K873, 115 for various emergencies and disaster projects 
the community will encounter.
Empowerment Grants and Loans
Youth, Women and Community Empowerment Loans and Grants have a total allocation of K5.8 million 
under the 2024 Council Budget. The Empowerment Loans make up 60 percent of the total allocation for 
Empowerment- that is, K3.5 million. On the other hand, Empowerment Grants are 40 percenmt of the 
total empowerment allocation- amounting to K2.3 million. 
Secondary School Boarding and Skills Development Bursaries
This component of the Constituency Development Programme has been allocated K5.8 million for the 
year 2024. This will help many less privileged children access better education in boarding schools and 
skills development will improve the capacities of the general population in the country.

Executive Authority:
Lufwanyama is a generally rural district and the Constituency Development Fund Committee (CDFC) has 
prioritised improvement of road infrustructure in the implementation of the 2024 CDF allocation of K30.6 
million. The key out put is the procurement of Heavy Equipment such as a Grader, an Excavator and a 
Lowbed truck. This entails that very little will be done in 2024 for Community Projects such as construction 
of schools and clinics. The procurement of additional 3, 313 School Desks in fulfilling the Presidential 
Pronouncements has been budgeted for in order to increase on the 5, 881 that have so far been procured 
since 2022. According to the guidance from the Ministry of Local Government and Riural Development 
(MLGRD), an allocation has been set aside for an Ambulance and Six (6) Motorbikes for Chiefs Retainers. The 
rest of the allocation under Community Projects is the Disaster Contingency. 
The Local Authority has also allocated K5.8 million for Empowement Grants and Loans (for Youth, Women 
and Community). This is coupled with a K5.8 million for Secondary School Boarding and Skills Development 
Bursaries. Lastly, K1.5 million is the Administrative Component.
