Director Finance informed the house that in 2024 the Local authority managed to achieve 
the following from the projects that were  proposed in 2023. 
1. Procurement of Water Bowser. 
2. Construction of phase-1 fire station. 
3. Creation of feeder roads in newly created plots. 
4. Purchase of the Gradder under CDF. 
5. Dister projects executed and others work is in progress. 
 
CHALLENGES FACED DURING THE YEAR 2024  
The facilitator informed the house that even though the Local authority managed to implement five 
projects that were proposed by the community, the following changes were faced: 
➢ Governments delayment in disbursing CDF consequently resulting in delay in 
implementation. 
➢ Governments delayment in disbursing the devolution support grant consequently 
resulting in delay in implementation. 
➢ The ministry guided that we budget for 10 million for the component of grant in lieu of 
rates, but at a later stage, it was guided that government could not fund us that amount 
therefore affecting the operations of the council.Hence Leading to adjustment of the 
budget downwards by the mentioned amount. This meant that all projects that were 
budgeted in the amount were to be suspended. 
PLENARY 
 
➢ Ward development Chairperson for Sokontwe raised a concern  why the Local  Authority 
delayed in completing of the CDF 2023 projects.  
In response, the Director of Finance informed the house that they was a delayment in the 
disbursement of and hence once the funds are received all the pending projects they will be 
completed. 

➢ Further, the stakeholders expressed concern regarding CDF Loan recoveries. 
In response the Director of Finance inform the stakeholders that the local authority is facin g 
challenges to recover the loans and the beneficiaries will be engaged to hear the challenges are 
facing and the way forward.

Proposed projects for 2025 
The Socio-economic Planner informed the members present that a number of applications were 
received from the community. However, resources were limited hence projects to be implemented 
in the FY 2025 would include:  
1. Procurement of Earth moving equipment 
2. Rehabilitation of Fungulwe feeder road 
3. Construction of Classroom Blocks 
4. Procurement of desks 
5. Construction of Milulu Health Post 
6. Drilling of commercial borehole Mukutuma Rural Health Centre 
7. Procurement of medical equipment  
 
Plenary 
Members of the house welcomed  the consideration of procuring more desks,  construct of 
classroom blocks, health post and medical equipment. However, the Local Authority was urged to 
buying a borehole drilling rig machine, because the water shortage was the greatest challenge the 
District was facing.  
In response, the Socio -economic planner indicated that the observation by the members was 
correct. However, resources were limited t o execute all the projects at once . Hence, the proposal 
would be considered in the next budget. 
Closing Remarks 
The Council treasurer thanked everyone for attending the meeting and further paid accolade to 
members of the committee for their overwhelming su pport rendered to management in the year 
2025, He encouraged members of the committee to exert more efforts in ensuring that the projects 
in the wards are submitted to the council for inclusion in the 2025 budget and to ensure that all 
projects were implemented in their wards. 
Closing prayer 
The meetings was closed in a word of prayer by Mr Christopher Kanyanta at 13:00 hrs.
